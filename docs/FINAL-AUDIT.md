## What changed and was verified

Shared issuer/scope/date/unit binding, comparative bank parsing, duration/date
normalisation, claim lineage and independently originated evidence checks were
strengthened. Retrieval now distinguishes publication date from actual availability
receipt and cannot backdate an undated source. Free-account preflight and durable
reservations prevent paid overage and uncertain-call retries. Worker checkpoints
retain reviewed evidence; UI creation idempotency, polling recovery, passage layout
and safe complete CSV exports were fixed. Locked Windows setup, managed local
services and a source-only draft archive support transfer without credentials.

320 backend tests passed, one existing Starlette deprecation warning; 14 Edge tests
passed and the production frontend built. Browser mocks and synthetic replay are
engineering validation only. Fresh installation, migrations, actual local health,
missing-key rejection and report persistence were checked. A current-source overlay
also passed frozen setup, npm clean install/build, migrations and dependency doctor.
No billing, plan upgrade, purchase, external deployment, push or submission occurred.

Manual source-page checks reproduced final ICICI's five and SBI's three reported
calculations, plus earlier HDFC/Axis/SBI observations. SOURCE-CHECKS.md documents
scope, dates, exact cells and caveats. HDFC merger comparisons are not organic
growth; net/plain advances are not gross. Five semantically verified cited claims
per company are **NOT VERIFIED** because no discovery was published. Automated
