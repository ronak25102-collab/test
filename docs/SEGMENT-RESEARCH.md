# Segment research repair

The 18 October Jio Financial screenshot was an actual insufficient-evidence
result, not a display-only failure: only consolidated revenue and net profit
were retained. Its downloaded exchange filing also contained named segment
revenue/results, but numeric rendering discarded the original section headings.
Repeated business names then lost their metric binding.

## Generic changes

- Repeat each original segment section heading beside its literal numeric row.
  Repeated names cannot conflate revenue, results, assets and liabilities.
- Extract named components and separately disclosed segment totals, retaining
  source currency, scale, exact duration, consolidated/standalone scope and IDs.
  Components labelled merely "all" are rejected as unnamed; consolidated profit
  cannot substitute for a segment schedule total.
- Register segment revenue contribution, reported segment-result contribution,
  and their difference. Supplied components must sum exactly to their reported
  total, with identical issuer, currency, normalized unit and reporting window.
  Loss-making segments remain negative. Ordinary cross-segment ratios remain
  forbidden; only these explicit reconciliation formulas permit components and
  their total in one calculation.
- Investigate a sufficiently distinct reconciled contribution relationship as a
  single-window observation. It cannot establish onset, persistence or cause.
  Counterevidence, novelty, numerical lineage and independent final review still
  decide publication. Missing comparable peers cap strength at Weak.
- Retrieve the immediately preceding year before more distant structured history.
  Do not spend numeric peer requests when no supported comparison target exists.
  A bare "Holdings" name no longer proves a financial business, including old
  directory false positives in peer selection.
- Prefer three financially anchored significance statements. Unsupported optional
  context can be removed only if three supported bullets remain, or two remain
  plus one already registered formula limitation. No new financial claim is
  invented. The headline, timeline and counterargument must already pass their
  numerical/anchor checks and remain unchanged. The entire cleaned draft gets
  fresh independent semantic review; adverse novelty or semantic verdicts cannot
  be bypassed by this local cleanup.
- Display diagnostic metric labels and dates in plain language. Source quotes and
  recorded evidence are unchanged.

## Financial interpretation limits

"Segment results" means the result as labelled in the cited segment schedule.
It must not be presented as consolidated net profit, cash profit or a causal
explanation. Gross segment revenue can include intersegment activity and differs
from consolidated revenue after eliminations. Source completeness, definitions
and reconciliations remain part of independent source and final claim review;
a matching arithmetic total alone does not establish economic comparability.

## Validation

Synthetic tests cover repeated section labels, loss-making segments, incorrect
net-revenue denominators, absent components, mismatched dates/scopes/entities,
unmatched segment names, unnamed components and rejection by independent review.
Browser tests verify readable gaps without relabelling an unverified result.

Read-only re-parsing of the originally downloaded filing now produces ten locally
bound candidates and ten registered calculations. A fresh cold live check also
independently verified ten facts and ten calculations, compared with the original
two facts and one calculation. That first validation exposed unanchored irrelevant
context in final drafting, which was correctly withheld; the bounded cleanup fix
was added afterward. The final live outcome is recorded separately. These local
checks do not establish a discovery until final publication review completes.

The full local backend suite passed 704 tests (one Windows symlink-permission skip); Ruff, the frontend production build and all 49 Edge browser tests passed.
