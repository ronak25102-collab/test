# Source policy

Only public HTTP(S) sources are eligible. Source bytes are hashed before
parsing, URLs are validated on every redirect, and private/loopback/link-local
destinations are blocked. Search snippets are leads and cannot be cited.

Issuer copies and exchange copies of the same filing share an origin family.
News that repeats an issuer filing is contextual, not independent corroboration.
The report exposes publication date, retrieval date, cutoff eligibility and
coverage failures. Full documents remain local; exports contain metadata,
short passages and hashes.
