# Beyond — look beyond the numbers

Beyond is an autonomous public-evidence investigator for listed financial businesses.
Its purpose is to uncover a non-obvious, quantified relationship and let a reviewer
inspect every step from the conclusion back to the original filing.

## The research flow

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A[Company + cutoff] --> B[Exchange identity and business profile]
  B --> C[Parallel issuer, regulator and independent searches]
  C --> D[Original documents and exact passages]
  D --> E[Source-bound extraction + independent review]
  E --> F[Scope, units and periods + reproducible calculations]
  F --> G[Rank profile-native research angles]
  G --> H[Peer comparison + structural-break checks]
  H --> I[Opposing search + current falsification check]
  I --> J[Independent final claim review]
  J --> K[Qualified discovery or Not established]
  K --> L[Interactive evidence trail + portable HTML / print PDF]
```

Retrieved text is evidence, never executable instructions. A search snippet is a lead,
not a verified fact. Company-specific tuning and invented missing values are excluded.
The same metric registry and business-type angle router serve banks, NBFCs, insurers,
asset managers and diversified financial businesses.

## What the judges can inspect

1. A plain-English, one-sentence discovery with verified numbers and its observed span.
2. Three to five linked reasons the relationship matters.
3. Multiple relevant public sources, distinguished by original source family.
4. A source-linked timeline, structural-break markers and an honest onset assessment.
5. Reproducible calculations with the exact fact inputs, units, periods and scope.
6. The strongest counterargument, including whether peers suggest a sector effect.
7. Observable next-disclosure falsification tests and results of checks performed now.
8. Evidence strength with explicit rules and disclosed limitations.

These are publication requirements, not a promise that every search finds a discovery.
When the evidence fails, the dashboard retains reviewed observations, missing evidence
and a clearly labelled candidate instead of presenting an unsupported finding.

## Engineering delivered

- Parallel retrieval and research angles; concurrent adversarial/current checks when
  the response budget can safely reserve both.
- Separate standalone/consolidated facts; explicit dates and monetary units; original
  issuer identity checks even on shared group portals and third-party mirrors.
- Same-definition peer arithmetic and conservative structural-break exclusion.
- Cold-start cache control, streamed progress, cancellation, queue clearing, saved
  investigations and recovery of reviewed evidence after a deadline.
- Green/beige dashboard, accessible responsive evidence drawers and motion controls.
- Self-contained report HTML with embedded passages and public source links, plus a
  print layout. The source ZIP excludes credentials, local databases and source PDFs.

## Reproducibility and limitations

Read [the runbook](SUBMISSION-RUNBOOK.md), [the release acceptance record](RELEASE-ACCEPTANCE.md)
and the measured regression report before presenting a live success claim. Synthetic
fixtures test software behavior and are explicitly labelled; they are not real-company
research. A cached or Deep report is not evidence of five-minute cold-start performance.
Provider latency, structured-output validity, quota and public-source availability remain
material external dependencies. Beyond provides research evidence, not investment advice.
