## Changes in this release

Independent initial searches and profile-native angles run concurrently. Current
reconciliation and adversarial review overlap only when their full response budgets
can both be reserved. Extraction stops on usable angle evidence, rather than on
unrelated profit calculations. Missing lending metrics remain in the NBFC focus.
Original percentage stock tables and dated column-oriented tables receive safer
source-bound extraction. Group-portal issuer identity and requested peer scope are
explicit. Unverified peer reports no longer inflate the model prompt. Optional
history preserves reasoning time and peer-source slots. An already reviewed valid
headline is reused verbatim, avoiding redundant writer/reviewer calls.

The dashboard adds saved investigations and truthful incomplete-run wording.
Reviewed Partial evidence survives a deadline during local publication, without
creating a late discovery. Provider errors include bounded field/type diagnostics;
financial scope, unit, period, exact-value and independent review gates remain.

## Acceptance limits

The short-headline validator, structural-break exclusion, peer comparability,
quarterly/onset stories, falsification checks, strength rules, formatting, portable
exports, queue controls and evidence recovery are implemented and tested. A real
successful cold discovery with two verified peers, adequate quarterly history,
an annotated verified break and a completed falsification review has not yet been
established by the recorded eight-company batch. Do not claim these live criteria
passed because their fixtures passed.

No real recovery-ratio percentage or company-specific causal mechanism is asserted
without verified inputs. Unknown filing dates stay unknown. Provider latency,
quota, malformed output, access restrictions and missing same-definition sources
remain risks. Retained partial reports and legacy cached reports keep their actual
mode, cutoff and outcome. Beyond is a research tool, not an investment recommendation.
